# Commercial hypothesis — not demand proof

## Hypothesis

Portfolio leaders in complex client-service organizations may pay for an evidence-bound review layer that exposes mismatches across commitments, staffing, WIP, financials, risk, and decision authority without replacing their systems of record.

## What is known

- The repository implements the complete synthetic decision workflow described in the candidate-authored blueprint.
- Deterministic controls can calculate health, enforce blocked-work completeness, prevent high-risk self-approval, invalidate dependent versions, and produce local receipts.

## What remains unknown

- Validated customer demand, willingness to pay, procurement requirements, integration priority, implementation cost, retention, outcomes, revenue, and competitive position.
- Whether any target employer or organization uses the represented operating model.

## Falsification test

The hypothesis weakens if five qualified portfolio operators cannot reproduce a meaningful cross-system decision using their own redacted inputs, or if an existing system of record can expose the same evidence chain without duplicate maintenance.
